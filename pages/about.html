About Me

Design Consultant at Slalom

My Journey

I'm Mason Pott, a design consultant at Slalom with a passion for creating beautiful, functional digital experiences that solve real problems.

My career has been driven by a belief that great design is more than aesthetics—it's about understanding user needs, business goals, and creating solutions that bridge that gap with elegance and clarity.

At Slalom, I work across diverse industries and organizations, helping teams transform their digital landscapes through strategic design thinking, user research, and innovative problem-solving.

What Drives Me

I'm energized by complex challenges that require creative solutions. Whether it's designing a new product, improving user experiences, or building design systems that scale, I bring a collaborative approach that values stakeholder input and data-driven decision making.

Outside of work, I'm passionate about design trends, emerging technologies, and exploring how design can create positive social impact.

Areas of Expertise

Strategic Design

Aligning design with business objectives to create compelling value propositions and competitive advantages.

User Experience

Crafting intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences through research-driven design.

Design Systems

Design Leadership

Mentoring teams, fostering design culture, and driving organizational transformation through design.
